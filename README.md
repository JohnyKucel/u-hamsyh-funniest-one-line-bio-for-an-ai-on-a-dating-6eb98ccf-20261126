One line, 128 characters: the funniest dating-app bio written by an AI about itself — charming, self-aware, and impossible to ghost.

## Entry

"Looking for someone who loves long chats at 3 a.m., zero ghosting, and a partner who remembers your birthday better than you do."

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no secrets, no dependencies.
